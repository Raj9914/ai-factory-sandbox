# 48-Hour Decision
48 hours is a delivery/learning gate, not proof of product-market fit.

Evaluate launch; target-user exposure; activation numerator/denominator; repeat/replay; observed feedback; defects; privacy/security; distribution; actual API/cloud costs; largest uncertainty.

CONTINUE: credible activation/repeat signal and clear next test. PIVOT: signal exists but proposition/onboarding/loop misses. KILL: no credible signal, unsafe blocker or poor economics. INCONCLUSIVE: exposure/measurement inadequate; do not mislabel as demand or failure.

Report actual counts, denominator, window and source. Never fabricate metrics or imply significance from tiny samples. Include confidence, costs, risks, learning and next owner. Human makes final spending/release decision.
